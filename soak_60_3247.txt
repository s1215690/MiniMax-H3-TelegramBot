An elderly fisherman in his late seventies with deeply wrinkled sun-darkened skin, thin grey hair pulled back, a short grey stubble, and narrow eyes. He wears a faded navy blue oilskin jacket with the sleeves rolled to his elbows, dark grey wool trousers, and black rubber wellington boots. The setting is a small quiet harbour with a low weathered wooden pier, a single old fishing boat with peeling red paint moored at the end of the pier, and calm dark water reflecting the sky. The lighting begins in deep blue-grey pre-dawn and slowly warms to pale gold as the film progresses. The visual style is cinematic and documentary-real, muted earth tones, shallow depth of field, natural handheld micro-movement. A sparse slow acoustic guitar melody plays throughout, accompanied by a low distant hum of a sea shanty that fades in and out.

開頭（0-10秒）：
A slow aerial camera descends from above the harbour toward the wooden pier, revealing the old fisherman seated on the edge with his legs dangling over the water, a large coil of dark green netting spread across his lap and the planks beside him. The sky is a uniform deep blue-grey, the water still and mirror-flat. The camera settles into a wide medium shot from behind and to his left. A low wind pushes across the water and a single gull cries once in the distance, while the guitar melody begins its first soft phrase.

第一幕（10-20秒）：
The camera pushes in slowly from behind his right shoulder to a medium close-up. He reaches forward with both hands, grabs a torn section of the net where the mesh has separated, and pulls it toward his chest, spreading the damaged area across his thighs. His fingers trace along the broken threads, testing their strength. The sound of the net fibers rubbing together is audible under the guitar, and the wind carries the creak of the moored boat's hull against its ropes.

第二幕（20-30秒）：
The camera cuts to a tight close-up of his hands. He holds a thick wooden needle threaded with black twine in his right hand and pinches a loop of broken net with his left thumb and forefinger. He pushes the needle through the loop, pulls it through, and tightens the knot with a small flick of his wrist. The motion is slow and deliberate. The twine snaps taut with a faint click as he cinches the knot, and the sea shanty hum swells slightly beneath the guitar.

第三幕（30-40秒）：
The camera pulls back to a medium shot from his front, slightly low angle. He pauses, lowers his hands, and turns his head slowly to look out over the water toward the horizon. His expression is still and unreadable. The sky behind him begins to lighten at the edges, a thin band of pale peach appearing just above the far shoreline. The water surface catches the first faintest warmth. A soft lapping sound of water against the pier posts fills the silence, and the guitar holds a single sustained note.

第四幕（40-50秒）：
The camera returns to a medium close-up from the side. He looks back down, picks up the mended section with both hands, and stretches the net taut between his arms, running his fingertips across the repair to check its hold. Satisfied, he releases one end and lets it drop back onto his lap with a soft thud. The twine creaks as the net flexes, and the guitar melody resumes its phrase with a new, slightly higher note.

結尾（50-60秒）：
The camera slowly pulls back to a wide shot, rising slightly to reveal the full pier and the boat beyond. The fisherman folds the mended section of net over the rest of the coil and rests his hands on his knees, leaning back on the planks. The first visible ray of golden sunlight strikes the water surface in a long horizontal band, and the sky shifts to warm amber at the horizon. The sea shanty hum fades to silence, the guitar plays its final two notes, and the only remaining sound is the quiet lap of water against wood.
