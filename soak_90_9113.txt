A lone astronaut in a white NASA-style pressure suit with a red patch on the left shoulder and gold-tinted visor stands inside a narrow cylindrical space station module. The walls are pale grey metal panels with thin blue indicator lights running along the ceiling. A large circular window at the far end of the module reveals deep black space and the slowly rotating Earth, its blue oceans and white cloud bands clearly visible, casting a soft cold blue glow into the module. The overall visual style is cinematic, desaturated with high contrast between the dark space and the glowing Earth. A single sustained low cello note with a faint ambient electronic hum runs beneath every scene for the entire duration.

開頭（0-10秒）：
The camera holds a wide static shot of the interior of the cylindrical module. The astronaut stands still at the center of the frame, facing the large circular window. Their breathing creates a faint rhythmic fog on the inside of the visor that clears with each exhale. The blue light from Earth illuminates the left side of their suit while the right side falls into shadow. The faint hum of life-support fans is audible beneath the cello.

第一幕（10-20秒）：
The astronaut takes one slow step forward, their magnetic boots releasing from the floor with a soft metallic click. They take a second step, their body swaying slightly in the low gravity as they drift closer to the window. The camera slowly pushes in from the wide shot to a medium shot, tightening the frame around the astronaut's torso and the window behind them. A low mechanical whir from the station's rotation is heard beneath the cello.

第二幕（20-30秒）：
The camera cuts to a tight close-up of the astronaut's face behind the gold visor. Their eyes are visible, wide and still, reflecting the curved blue surface of Earth. A single drop of condensation forms at the edge of the visor and slides down slowly. The cello note sustains and a very quiet electronic pulse begins to layer beneath it like a heartbeat.

第三幕（30-40秒）：
The camera pulls back to a medium shot from behind the astronaut's right shoulder. They raise their right gloved hand slowly and press the flat of their palm against the inside of the circular window glass. The glass vibrates faintly under the pressure. Their reflection is superimposed over the Earth's surface. The sound of the glove pressing against glass produces a soft muffled thud, and the cello deepens slightly in register.

第四幕（40-50秒）：
The camera shifts to a direct view through the window, looking outward at Earth. The planet rotates slowly from right to left. A large white cloud system drifts across the African continent. The terminator line between day and night is visible as a thin dark crescent on the left edge of the frame. The ambient hum of the station is louder here, a constant low-frequency drone beneath the cello.

第五幕（50-60秒）：
The camera returns to the astronaut's face in extreme close-up, the visor now showing the Earth filling the entire reflection. Their eyes blink once, slowly. The blue light intensifies as the rotation brings a sunlit ocean directly in front of the window, washing the interior with a brighter glow. A single high-pitched electronic tone enters the mix, thin and distant, like a signal from far away.

第六幕（60-70秒）：
The camera slowly orbits around the astronaut in a wide arc, revealing the full length of the module. The astronaut remains still with their hand on the window. The blue light from Earth sweeps across the metal wall panels as the station's slow rotation shifts the angle. Faint rivets and warning labels on the walls pass through the light. The cello is joined by a second, higher cello note forming a simple minor interval.

第七幕（70-80秒）：
The astronaut slowly lowers their right hand from the window and takes two steps backward, their boots clicking against the metal floor. They turn their head slightly to the left, looking back toward the camera. The visor catches a brief reflection of the station's interior lights. The electronic pulse beneath the cello fades, leaving only the sustained note.

結尾（80-90秒）：
The camera holds a final wide static shot identical to the opening framing. The astronaut stands motionless at the center of the module, facing the window. Earth continues its slow rotation, a new cloud band entering the frame from the right. The blue glow remains constant. The cello note holds and then slowly fades to silence over the final three seconds, leaving only the faint hum of the life-support fans.
