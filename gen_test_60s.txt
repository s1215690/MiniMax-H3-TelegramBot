A woman in her early thirties stands alone on a wet train platform at night. She has shoulder-length dark hair damp with rain, a long charcoal-grey wool coat buttoned to the chin, a thin black scarf wrapped twice around her neck, and dark ankle boots. She carries no bag. The setting is a narrow elevated platform with a glass shelter at the far end, its overhead fluorescent lights casting a flat greenish-white glow onto the rain-slicked concrete. Beyond the platform edge, darkness swallows the tracks except where distant streetlights pierce the mist. The visual style is cinematic and muted, with desaturated blues and greys, shallow depth of field, and a slow contemplative pace. A single low cello note holds a sustained drone beneath the entire film, joined by the constant sound of rain.

開頭（0-5秒）：
A wide static shot frames the empty platform from above at a slight angle. Rain streaks fall in diagonal lines through the fluorescent light. The woman stands motionless near the yellow safety line, her coat shoulders slightly hunched, her gaze fixed on the dark tunnel mouth where the train will arrive. The camera holds still. The sound of steady rain on glass and concrete fills the frame, with the low cello drone beneath.

第一幕（5-15秒）：
The camera slowly pushes in from a medium-wide to a medium shot of the woman's upper body. She lifts her right hand and tucks a strand of wet hair behind her ear, then lowers her arm back to her side. Her breath fogs faintly in the cold air. Her eyes remain fixed forward, unblinking. A drop of water runs down the glass shelter behind her and falls to the floor. The cello drone continues, and a distant rumble of approaching rails begins to build beneath the rain.

第二幕（15-25秒）：
The camera cuts to a low angle looking down the platform toward the tunnel mouth. A warm white headlight appears in the distance, growing larger and brighter. The woman's reflection appears in the wet concrete floor, distorted and trembling. She does not shift her weight. The rumble intensifies into a deep mechanical groan. Rain continues to fall in the foreground, out of focus.

第三幕（25-35秒）：
The train slides into the station, its silver carriages catching the platform lights in long horizontal streaks. The woman steps back half a pace from the yellow line as the train decelerates. The doors open with a soft pneumatic hiss. Three passengers step out onto the platform and walk past her toward the shelter, their footsteps splashing. The camera tracks the train's motion with a slow lateral pan. The cello drone is joined by the rising hum of the train's electrical systems.

第四幕（35-45秒）：
The camera settles on a medium close-up of the woman's face and shoulders. She watches the open doors. A man in a yellow raincoat boards the train and disappears inside. Her fingers curl slightly at her sides, then uncurl. Her jaw tightens. She does not step forward. The fluorescent light flickers once above her, casting a brief shadow across her eyes. The cello note bends slightly upward, a microtonal shift that adds tension.

第五幕（45-55秒）：
The doors begin to close. The woman's reflection is visible in the dark window glass between the doors, her face pressed close to the glass as if she is behind it. The doors seal with a soft metallic click. The train lurches forward and accelerates, the carriages sliding out of frame. The woman remains exactly where she stood, her body perfectly still, her coat rippling faintly in the draft from the departing train. The cello drone drops to a lower register.

結尾（55-60秒）：
The camera slowly pulls back to the same wide high angle as the opening shot. The platform is empty except for the woman, who now lowers her head and presses both palms flat against her thighs. The rain continues without pause. The last note of the cello fades into the sound of rain alone.
