An old fisherman in his late sixties with deep weathered skin, a thin white beard, and salt-stained hands. He wears a faded navy wool sweater with the sleeves pushed to his elbows, a grey flat cap, and worn brown work trousers tucked into rubber boots. Setting: a small stone harbour with a wooden quay, a single weathered fishing boat moored at the edge, calm dark water reflecting the first light of dawn, low fog clinging to the surface. Visual style: naturalistic, warm amber and soft blue dawn palette, shallow depth of field in close-ups, 35mm film grain. Music: a slow, sparse piano melody with soft sustained strings, minimal and contemplative, playing throughout the entire film at low volume.

開頭（0-10秒）：
A wide static shot of the harbour at dawn. The sky is a pale gradient from deep blue at the top to soft amber near the horizon. Low fog drifts across the dark water. The old fisherman sits at the far end of the wooden quay, small and still, his back slightly hunched. A single boat bobs gently beside him. The camera holds this wide frame without movement for the full duration. A low, steady breeze moves the fog in slow horizontal streaks across the water surface.

第一幕（10-20秒）：
The camera slowly pushes in from the wide shot toward the fisherman, who is now clearly visible sitting on a wooden crate at the quay's edge. He lifts a section of tangled green fishing net with both hands, shakes it once to straighten the strands, and lets it fall back onto his lap. His fingers find a torn section near the edge. He leans forward slightly, his cap tilting down over his brow. The camera settles into a medium shot at chest height. The gentle creak of the boat's hull against the mooring rope and the soft slap of water against the quay stones fill the scene beneath the piano melody.

第二幕（20-30秒）：
A tight close-up of the fisherman's hands on the net. His right thumb presses flat against a torn knot while his left hand threads a short length of twine through the gap. He pulls the twine taut with a sharp flick of his wrist, then wraps it twice around the knot and tucks the end underneath his thumbnail. His knuckles are swollen and the skin is cracked and dry. The camera drifts almost imperceptibly to the right, revealing the texture of the coarse green mesh. The dry rasp of twine sliding through rope fibre and the soft wet click of a loose knot tightening are audible over the sustained string note.

第三幕（30-40秒）：
The camera pulls back to a medium shot. The fisherman stops working, lets his hands rest on his knees, and tilts his head upward to look out over the water. A thin line of orange light has now broken along the horizon. His expression is still and unreadable. A small wave laps against the hull of the boat beside him, and the boat rocks a few centimetres. He blinks slowly, then lowers his gaze back to the net in his lap and picks up his twine again. The water lapping sound grows slightly louder for a moment as the wave hits, then fades back to a soft rhythmic hush.

第四幕（40-50秒）：
The camera moves to a slightly low angle from the water's surface, looking up at the fisherman's hands as they resume mending. He pulls the mended section of net upward with both hands, stretching it between his arms to inspect the repair. The green mesh catches the rising light and glows faintly. He nods once, a small almost imperceptible movement of his chin. His right hand releases the net and he lets it settle back down. The camera holds on his hands as they come to rest. The twine gives a final short snap as the knot tightens, and the piano melody introduces a new, slightly brighter note.

結尾（50-60秒）：
The camera slowly pulls back and rises, returning to the wide establishing shot. The fisherman stands from his crate, rolls the mended net into a tight bundle with both arms, and tucks it under his left arm. He turns and walks along the quay toward his moored boat, his boots making a soft rhythmic thud on the wet wood. The sun is now a visible disc above the horizon, painting the fog gold. He reaches the boat's gunwale and places the net inside. The camera holds as he stands at the edge of the quay, looking back toward open water. His boots thud steadily on the wooden planks, and the piano melody resolves on a single sustained chord that fades slowly into the sound of lapping water and distant gulls.
