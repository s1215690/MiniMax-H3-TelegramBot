The light is pre-dawn: a deep indigo blue overhead, with a narrow band of amber and soft pink appearing at the far horizon, slowly widening. The visual style is naturalistic and cinematic, with a muted cool-to-warm color grade, shallow depth of field, and a slow, contemplative pace.
